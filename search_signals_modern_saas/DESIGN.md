## Brand & Style

This design system expresses algorithmic clarity, modern search intelligence, and actionable data visibility. Designed for enterprise marketers, technical SEO analysts, and digital strategists, the experience balances analytical authority with effortless digital lightness. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Corporate / Modern Minimalist** principles elevated by the proprietary visual motif: **Search Signals**. Search Signals introduce subtle node networks, crawling trajectories, connected trace lines, and pulse points into the interface. Rather than creating decorative noise, these elements illustrate structural intelligence—such as indexed paths, SERP ranks, and site graph architectures.

Surfaces remain airy, luminous, and spacious, utilizing cool ice tones paired against crisp deep navy typography and electric primary blue drivers. Layouts feel fast, deliberate, and mathematically aligned, inspiring complete confidence in mission-critical data.

## Colors

The palette relies on deliberate atmospheric layering, stepping from ultra-light ice tints down to deep navy contrast:

- **Primary Brand (`#4583E8`):** The primary engine color. Reserved strictly for primary calls-to-action, active navigational indicators, key highlight nodes, and confirmed interactive states.
- **Secondary Accent (`#7BA3ED`):** Interactive utility blue used for supporting action buttons, dynamic chart vector lines, interactive icons, and sub-metric focus.
- **Tertiary Structural Blue (`#B6CDF6`):** Decorative signal paths, grid traces, active dividers, and emphasized metric border tags.
- **Surface Canvas (Ice Blue `#F0FAFF`):** The default application canvas and section backdrop, delivering a cool, modern atmosphere instead of stark neutral white.
- **Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity elevated containers resting over Ice Blue.
- **Surface Hover & Wells (Cloud Blue `#D5EBFB`):** Hover fills, secondary card layers, data pill backdrops, and active code or table cell backgrounds.
- **Text & Hierarchy:**
  - **Headings & High Contrast Anchors (`#0B1F3A`):** Deep Navy for crisp, high-impact titles, dark cards, and deep structural footers.
  - **Primary Body (`#14213D`):** Rich, legible slate-navy for all body and tabular values.
  - **Secondary Text (`#64748B`):** Refined slate for metadata, captions, timestamps, and column labels.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E5EAF2`):** Subtle hairline borders separating content panels without heavy visual framing.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - **Success (`#10B981`):** Indexation complete, positive SERP movement, healthy site health score.
  - **Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Crawl warnings, redirects, canonical conflicts.
  - **Critical (`#EF4444`):** Broken links, 4xx/5xx response codes, de-indexed paths.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve a contemporary, geometrically refined, yet highly legible presentation suited for tech-forward SaaS. 

- **Display & Headings:** Use tight negative letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (Bold to ExtraBold) paired with `#0B1F3A` Deep Navy to assert authority and visual presence.
- **Body Content:** Set with regular weight (`400`) at comfortable line heights (1.5x–1.6x) in `#14213D` to ensure prolonged readability during complex analytical workflows.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Utilize medium-to-bold weights with slight positive letter tracking (`0.01em` to `0.04em`) in all-caps or title case for metric headers, status badges, and signal node indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict **12-column responsive fluid grid** with generous safe margins to create an expansive, breathable marketing and dashboard canvas.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout, `margin-lg` (64px) outer gutters, `gutter` (24px) column spacing. Standard max content containment is 1280px.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout, `margin-md` (32px) margins, `gutter-sm` (16px) gutters. Complex tables shift to horizonal card lists or scroll containers.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-sm` (16px) margins, `gutter-sm` (16px) gutters. Multi-column metric groups collapse into vertical stacks or swipeable carousels.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence relies on 8px base increments. Sections alternate between standard padding (`space-xl` / 40px) and double section padding (80px) to distinguish analytical tools from product storytelling blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via clean planar surfaces and **diffused ambient blue shadows**, avoiding heavy muddy drops:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F0FAFF` Ice Blue base layer. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Data Panels):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px border of `#E5EAF2` with a subtle blue-tinted elevation: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(69, 131, 232, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Focus):** Border transitions to `#B6CDF6`. Elevation expands to `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(69, 131, 232, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Command Menus):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.18)`.
- **Search Signals Layering:** Trace vectors, crawl nodes, and connecting path lines operate between Level 0 and Level 1 via SVG overlays using `#B6CDF6` at 40%–70% opacity, punctuated by glowing nodes colored `#4583E8` with a 4px soft bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(69, 131, 232, 0.5)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded (`2`)** curvature profile (base radius: `0.5rem` / 8px). 

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Chips:** Set to `0.5rem` (8px) for a modern, approachable feel.
- **Standard Cards & Data Modules:** Configured with `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) to clearly frame complex SEO metrics and data tables.
- **Large Hero Panels & Modals:** Use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- **Search Signal Nodes:** Circular (`50%` / 9999px) terminal points connected by 1.5px and 2px path strokes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background `#4583E8`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, radius 8px (`0.5rem`), padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover state shifts to `#3B72CD` with subtle lift. Active state compresses scale to `0.98`.
- **Secondary Button:** Background `#D5EBFB`, text `#14213D`, border 1px solid `#B6CDF6`. Hover triggers background `#B6CDF6` with transition.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Button:** Background transparent, text `#4583E8`, hover background `#F0FAFF`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#EF4444`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards & Data Modules
- **Default Card:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E5EAF2` perimeter border, 16px corner radius, padding `space-lg` (24px).
- **Signal-Augmented Card:** Subtle top or side border accent (2px `#4583E8`), optional corner watermark showing connected vector nodes.
- **Metric Highlight Card:** Features large numeral display (`headline-lg`), metadata in `#64748B`, and a dedicated trend chip.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E5EAF2`, border-radius 8px, text `#14213D`, placeholder `#64748B`. Focus state: border `#4583E8`, outline: 3px solid rgba(69, 131, 232, 0.2).
- **Domain/URL Input Bar (Hero Search):** Elevated card enclosure containing input field, lead-in search node icon, and embedded primary submit button. Height 56px, corner radius 12px.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Fully rounded (`9999px`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, font `label-sm`.
  - **Index Active / Growth:** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`, text `#10B981`.
  - **Warning / Issue:** Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`, text `#F59E0B`.
  - **Critical / Error:** Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`, text `#EF4444`.
  - **Keyword Metric / Neutral:** Background `#D5EBFB`, text `#14213D`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Control Box:** 18px x 18px, border 1.5px solid `#B6CDF6`, border-radius 4px (checkbox) or 50% (radio), background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Checked State:** Background `#4583E8`, border-color `#4583E8`, with white checkmark or center pip. Focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(69, 131, 232, 0.25)`.

### Lists & SERP Results
- **SERP Preview List:** White background, divided by 1px `#E5EAF2` bottom border. Title in `headline-sm` with hover color `#4583E8`. URL breadcrumb path rendered in `#64748B` with subtle right chevrons. Body snippet in `body-sm`.

### Search Signals (Signature Component)
- **Node Paths:** SVG-driven polyline trees representing site architectures and crawl depth. Lines are stroked at 1.5px with `#B6CDF6`. Active crawl indicators are 6px circular nodes filled with `#4583E8` with a pulsing outer ring of `rgba(69, 131, 232, 0.3)`.
